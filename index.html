Frequently asked questions

A few things to know before you begin.

Which platforms are shown?

The interface includes YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest and TikTok. Actual processing depends on the backend service and each platform's current rules.

Why doesn't the Continue button download a video yet?

This file is the complete animated front-end design. A secure server-side downloader/API still needs to be connected to fetch media and provide download files. The page does not pretend that a download succeeded.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The layout adapts to mobile, tablet and desktop screens.

Can I download any video?

Only download content you own or have permission to save, and follow the source platform's terms, copyright rules and applicable laws. Some platforms restrict downloading or require creator permission.
